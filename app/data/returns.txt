ShopFlow Returns

Customers can return most products within 30 days after delivery.

Products must generally be unused and returned in their original packaging.

Some products may be excluded from the return policy for hygiene or regulatory reasons.

Customers can start a return from the Orders section.

After a return request is approved, ShopFlow provides return instructions.

Refunds are processed after the returned product has been received and inspected.

The refund is sent to the original payment method.
