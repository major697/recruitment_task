ShopFlow Orders

Customers can cancel an order as long as the order has not entered the shipping process.

Orders that are already being prepared for shipment cannot be cancelled through the customer dashboard.

If an order cannot be cancelled, the customer can wait until delivery and then use the return process.

Customers can view the current status of their order in the Orders section of the ShopFlow dashboard.

Order statuses include:
- Pending
- Confirmed
- Processing
- Preparing for shipment
- Shipped
- Delivered
- Cancelled

A cancelled order does not generate a shipping charge.
