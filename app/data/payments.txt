ShopFlow Payments

ShopFlow supports credit cards, debit cards and PayPal.

Credit card payments are authorized immediately after checkout.

If payment authorization fails, the order remains in Pending status.

Customers are not charged when payment authorization fails.

For successful credit card payments, the amount is captured when the order is confirmed.

Refunds are returned to the original payment method.

Credit card refunds usually appear within 3 to 7 business days.
